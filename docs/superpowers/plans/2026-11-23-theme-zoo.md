## Global Constraints

- Item rules: `docs/game/sculpting.md`. Footprints, ids, Thai names, categories and batches exactly as `docs/game/items-zoo.md`; its content rules (friendly cartoon animals, resting big cats, no prison-like cages, no red cross).
- School and amusement unchanged: their geometry snapshots pass without regeneration.
- Draw calls with a full zoo map stay in the existing range (smooth ≈ 15/17 with floors, pretty ≈ 48/50); 60 fps on the Mac real-GPU headless check.
- `src/models/*` are controller-owned (no changes expected).
- Owner rules for browser checks: Playwright's bundled headless Chromium only (no `channel`; `--use-angle=metal --ignore-gpu-blocklist --enable-gpu` for real-GPU numbers), one launch per script, never headed, never the real Chrome app or chrome-devtools MCP; no per-item sweeps — batch gallery day + night only.

## Review Focus
1. Animals read as the named animal from the default camera (size, colour, one or two key features).
2. Moving parts (pony-ride spin, parrot perch, monkey rope, sway crowns) share pivots and never cut through static parts.
3. The zoo mix (19/17/14/10) still lets a planning player fill most of the map (simulation).
4. A saved School or amusement city still loads; theme select works for all three.
5. Items stay inside their footprint and over the 0.02 ground-surface rule.
